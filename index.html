The control plane

Every change leaves
a trail.

AppPilot connects the product surface to the engineering systems that make change safe. No black box. No leap of faith.

01Persistent context

README, architecture, schema, conventions, and test strategy stay attached to the application across every change, not just the first one.

02Isolated execution

Every change runs in a workspace that can be inspected, tested, and discarded.

03Human control

Plans, diffs, findings, approvals, and promotion history stay visible to the team.
